## A. D24-A rotations, linear head: per-subject differences and two-stage bootstrap

Per-subject difference = mean over the rotation's 3 seeds of (ROC arm a - ROC arm b) within that subject. Pooled = the D24 estimand (per-rotation seed mean of the pooled-fold contrast, then mean over rotations). Wilcoxon and sign-flip: over subjects (independence across subjects assumed; subjects in one fold share a trained probe). Mixed-model alternative (statsmodels absent): exact rotation-block sign-flip on the 5 rotation means of the subject differences, and the t-interval over those means (df 4). Two-stage bootstrap: per rotation, resample its test subjects with replacement, recompute every run's pooled ROC, then resample the 3 seeds; mean over the 5 rotations; 10,000 reps. Rotation bootstrap = the D24-D algorithm (rotations, then seeds) rerun here for comparison. TOST +/-3 uses a 90% CI; it is read on two intervals because they answer different questions: the two-stage bootstrap holds the 5 trained folds fixed (test-set uncertainty of the D24 estimand), the t-interval over rotation means (the mixed-model alternative) also carries between-fold variation of the per-subject differences. The verdict column says which of them shows equivalence.

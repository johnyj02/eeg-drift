- **F11** `fig_effects`: Paired adaptation effects under the fixed probe recipe and tuned encoder-level linear probes (ROC-AUC x100, positive = the first arm scores higher). Two panels per model compare last - lp (black circle, the SA gain) and last - last_noise (pink triangle, the noise control). Open markers show the fixed recipe; filled markers show tuned probes. Whiskers are 95% Nadeau-Bengio corrected intervals over five rotation means for CHB-MIT, Siena and filtered Siena, or ordinary t-intervals over five seeds on DL's fixed split (seed variation conditional on the same patients). All intervals are checked against artifacts/analysis/D31/d31.json, using its test/train ratios. Rows are raw and train-standardized features. Zero is the thick line; the dotted lines mark -3 and +3, the ROC equivalence margin. Equivalence decisions use TOST, not containment of these 95% intervals. A missing marker means that the arm was not run. The random-feature comparisons remain in the full result tables; absolute arm levels are in F12. Fixed-recipe SA gains above three points, compared with the corresponding tuned estimates: BIOT Siena, raw features +9.2 against +1.7; BIOT Siena filtered, raw features +4.1 against -0.1; BIOT DL's split, q95, raw features +8.8 against +0.6. Sources: artifacts/analysis/D31/d31.json (primary tuned analysis and matched fixed-recipe readings); fixed-recipe arm values checked against D27B/d27b.json, D27/cpu.json and stats/stats.json.
- **F12** `fig_absolute`: Absolute test ROC-AUC of every arm with the fixed probe recipe and with tuned probes (encoder-level linear probe; ROC-AUC x100), so that a difference between two poor arms is visible. Two panels per model, the fixed recipe (open markers) and the tuned probe (filled markers), on one shared scale; within a row, top to bottom, lp (blue circle), last (orange square), last_noise (pink triangle down), lp_randinit (amber diamond) and last_randinit (green triangle up). Each marker is the mean over the 5 units (rotation means of 3 seeds, or 5 seeds on DL's split); the dotted line at 50 is chance. Rows per setting: raw features and standardized features. Settings: CHB-MIT, Siena and Siena filtered under the revised protocol (5 subject rotations, 3 seeds each, overlap labels), and DL's split (upstream labels, 5 seeds on one split) with standard input for both models and with BIOT's q95 input (BIOT only). A missing marker means that arm was not run in that cell (no noise arm for most fixed-recipe cells, no random-init arm with q95 input). The arms the fixed recipe misreads: BIOT with q95 input on raw features, lp / last / last_noise 33.1 / 41.9 / 33.3 with the fixed recipe against 72.5 / 73.1 / 73.8 with the tuned probe, and frozen BIOT on Siena (raw features) 51.3 against 73.6. Sources: artifacts/analysis/D31/d31.json (tuned probes and their fixed-recipe partners, same seeds); the fixed-recipe arm means equal artifacts/analysis/D27B/d27b.json and artifacts/analysis/D27/cpu.json (last_noise: E27 raw, A6 standardized, A1 q95 input), which are the arm means of artifacts/tables/d24-*.md (checked).
